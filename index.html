The 6 Pillars Explained

Operational Excellence

Run and monitor systems to deliver business value, continually improving processes (IaC, CI/CD, Monitoring).

Security

Protect data, systems, and assets. Use IAM, encryption, and automated threat detection.

Reliability

Recover from disruptions, dynamically acquire computing resources (Multi-AZ, Auto Scaling).

Performance Efficiency

Use computing resources efficiently to meet system requirements (Serverless, Right-sizing).

Cost Optimization

Avoid unnecessary costs. Pay only for what you need (Auto Scaling, Spot instances).

Sustainability

Minimize environmental impacts of running cloud workloads (Max utilization, managed services).
